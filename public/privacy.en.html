2. Usage statistics

Only daily totals are counted: how many people were active, how many scans, deals and notifications there were, and how often each chain's deals were tapped. This happens on the app's own server, with no third-party analytics and no tracking cookies. It does not record list contents, locations, IP addresses or who tapped what.

3. Who data is shared with

  • Your household sees the shared shopping list and who added or bought items. The admin sees every user's name, household and last city.
  • Hosting: Oracle Cloud, Israel (Jerusalem) region. The European Commission recognises Israel as providing adequate data protection.
  • Location and addresses: sent to OpenStreetMap Nominatim to find the city and country. Your name is not sent.
  • Deals in Austria and Germany: the server sends the search terms from your list and the postcode to marktguru to search store flyers. Your name and exact location are not sent.
  • Translation: items not written in the local language may be sent to the MyMemory translation service. Your name and location are not sent.
  • Adding from a photo: the barcode is read from the photo on your device, and the photo itself is never sent anywhere. Only the barcode number is sent by the server to the free Open Food Facts product database to find the product name.
  • Notifications: the content (product, price, chain) passes through the push service of your browser or device maker (e.g. Google or Apple).
  • Dictation: speech recognition (🎤) is done by your browser or operating system; on some devices your voice is processed by the maker. The app never receives or stores recordings.
  • Otherwise data is not sold, rented or shared, unless the law requires it.

4. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash (scrypt). Each user's data is available only to them and their household.

5. Retention

Data is kept while the account exists. When an account is deleted, its personal data is removed from the server. Anonymous totals are kept for about 13 months at most.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict, export or object to the processing of your data. You can delete your account yourself at any time: Settings → “My account” → “Delete my account”. Contact: [email]. You may also complain to a data protection authority (in Austria: www.dsb.gv.at).

7. Children

The app is meant for people aged 16 and over. Younger people may use it only with a parent's consent.

8. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the app will ask you to accept the new version.
